## Workflow

draft JDs & job ads (Claude/ChatGPT) → support screening (with care — see gotcha) → interview prep & scheduling → policy & comms drafting → engagement/sentiment analysis.

## Core stack

Claude/ChatGPT (drafting) · an ATS with AI · a survey tool.

## Verdict

drafting and analysis are strong wins; candidate screening is the danger zone.

## Gotchas

AI ranking of candidates risks bias and legal exposure — humans decide; never put sensitive personal data into consumer tools; follow employment law.
