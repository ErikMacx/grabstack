## Workflow

research (Perplexity) → deliverables & docs (Claude/ChatGPT) → decks (a deck tool) → admin & comms automation (Cowork) → finance basics (Claude for Excel).

## Core stack

Claude or ChatGPT (the all-rounder) · Perplexity · a deck tool.

## Verdict

a small team can punch far above its weight — pick one strong generalist model and go deep, rather than spreading across many.

## Gotchas

client confidentiality — check data terms; review before billing AI-drafted work as bespoke.
