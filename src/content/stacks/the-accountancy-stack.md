## Workflow

categorise & enter data (AI bookkeeping) → reconcile → report & narrate (Claude) → client comms → research tax/reg (verify everything).

## Core stack

an AI-enabled accounting platform · Claude for Excel/reporting.

## Verdict

automating the grunt work frees accountants for advisory — the higher-value seat.

## Gotchas

never rely on AI for tax or regulatory facts unverified; audit the numbers; client-data privacy and professional rules bind you.
