Oura Ring 5 went on sale in June 2026 at $399 (premium finishes cost $100 more, and the membership is $5.99 a month). It is about 40% smaller than earlier Oura rings and adds Health Radar features such as Blood Pressure Signals and Nighttime Breathing. Oura filed publicly to raise up to $2.2B at about a $15B valuation, then shelved the IPO indefinitely on 29 September 2026, citing market uncertainty. Ultrahuman's Ring Pro returned to the US from May 2026 after the import ban, and Samsung has confirmed a Galaxy Ring 2 is in development, expected in early 2027; no new information was found on Muse's Ring One.
