Midjourney's own version notes list V8.2 (24 July 2026) as the default, describing its images as "more creative, bold, sophisticated, and edgy", with a new Edit Model replacing the previous reference tools. It builds on V8.1 (14 April 2026), which brought 2K HD generation and made standard jobs about four to five times faster. The V8 models do not appear on the Artificial Analysis text-to-image leaderboard, whose newest Midjourney entry is V7 Alpha, so there is no independent ranking behind its reputation for stylised, artistic images. The Edit Model matters most if you need to refine one image rather than re-roll until you get lucky.
