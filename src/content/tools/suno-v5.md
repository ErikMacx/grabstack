Suno v6 comes as v6 and v6-wild for Pro and Premier subscribers and a faster v6-mini for everyone, and adds editing of specific song sections, mashups and text, audio, image and video inputs. Around it Suno shipped Studio 2.0, its browser-based DAW (13 August), a Speech beta that generates narration and a backing score in one track (1 October) and Albums (5 October), and signed partnerships with BMG (12 August) and Believe and TuneCore (8 September), after settling with Warner in November 2025. Universal and Sony's second lawsuit, filed in Boston federal court on 18 September, argues that training v6 on the outputs of earlier, allegedly infringing models "launders" the infringement, while their first case from 2024 is still in discovery. Suno now reports more than 2 million subscribers, but treat commercial use of its output as legally unsettled.
