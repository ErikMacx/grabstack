Manus Desktop brings Manus's general-purpose agent to the user's own computer. The main change since June is corporate: Meta's December 2025 agreement to buy Manus, reportedly for US$2–3 billion, was blocked by China's National Development and Reform Commission on 27 April 2026; Meta said on 15 June that it was cutting ties, and Manus announced on 11 August that it would operate as an independent company.
