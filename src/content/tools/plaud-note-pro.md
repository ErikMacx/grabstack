Plaud Note Pro is a credit-card-sized recorder for calls and in-person meetings that transcribes and summarises through Plaud's app, with 300 free transcription minutes a month and paid plans above that. Since June Plaud has moved beyond recorders: it announced Plaud One, AI earbuds with an eSIM charging case ($249 pre-order, shipping Q4 2026), shipped MCP connectors so Claude and ChatGPT can read your recordings (22 September 2026), and on 30 September added Plaud Agent for teams, which drafts emails and documents from meetings through connectors such as Slack, Gmail and Notion, paid for with new Plaud Credits. The company reported 2.5 million users and $100M annual run-rate revenue as of June 2026.
