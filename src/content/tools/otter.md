Otter transcribes and summarises meetings with live captions and chat across your meeting history, and says it has over 35 million users. In April 2026 it added an MCP server so ChatGPT and Claude can query meetings, plus connectors for Google Drive, Jira, Salesforce and Notion. On 21 July 2026 it launched Live Assist, an Enterprise-only agent that shows private tip cards during Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls, grounded in your own playbooks and past meetings. Otter's bot is one of those Microsoft now identifies in Teams, where detected bots wait in the lobby for organiser approval by default.
