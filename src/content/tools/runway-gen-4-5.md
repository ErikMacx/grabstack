Gen-4.5 (December 2025) is still Runway's flagship generator, with no successor announced, and it does not appear among the top models on Artificial Analysis's current video leaderboards. Since June, Runway has built around it: an upgraded Act-Two for motion capture (17 July), Ruby for turning SDR video into HDR (20 August), plugins for Premiere Pro and After Effects (8 September) and ProRes and PNG-sequence exports (23 September). It also hosts other makers' models, including Gemini Omni Flash, Seedance 2.5, Wan 3.0 and MiniMax Hailuo 3.0, and in July added Model Routers to its API to pick a model by cost, speed or quality. For builders, Runway is now less a single-model bet and more a production layer over several model makers.
