Beam AI describes itself as an agentic automation platform that turns business processes into agents without code. Its site lists 1,000+ integrations, deployment in the cloud or on-premises, and SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliance. Its accuracy and savings figures (such as 98% accuracy over 10 million tasks) are its own claims. Hebbia, founded in 2020, makes Matrix, which answers natural-language questions over documents with linked citations for finance and legal teams. The latest funding round we could confirm is a $130 million Series B in 2024 (Wikipedia). We could not open a primary source for any later round. Hebbia's site shows Matrix as a grid of companies and research fields linked to source filings, and lists no pricing or funding. No other material change found since 2026-10-07.
