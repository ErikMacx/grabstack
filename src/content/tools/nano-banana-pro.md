Nano Banana Pro is no longer Google's headline image model: the Gemini app now calls Nano Banana 2 (Gemini 3.1 Flash Image) its "latest state of the art image model", and offers Pro to Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers through a "Redo with Pro" option. Both models reached general availability in the Gemini API on 28 May 2026, and a cheaper Nano Banana 2 Lite followed on 30 June. On Artificial Analysis, Pro (Elo 1,102) now sits behind Nano Banana 2 (1,125) and well behind GPT Image 2.5 (1,197), so its June lead is gone. For most builders Nano Banana 2 is the better default, as it ranks higher and costs about half as much per image.
