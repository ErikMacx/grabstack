Granola records meeting audio on your own device, so no bot appears in the participant list, and merges the transcript with your own notes. It raised $125M led by Index Ventures at a $1.5B valuation in March 2026, and already offers an MCP server and APIs so other AI tools can use your meeting context. Since June it has launched an Android app (1 July), an Apple Watch app (28 July) and support for 32 languages (2 September). The bot-free approach is now being copied: ChatGPT's Meetings plugin (September 2026) takes notes from the desktop app, and Microsoft Teams is making visible bots harder to use.
