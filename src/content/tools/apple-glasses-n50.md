Apple's smart glasses, code-named N50, are still unannounced. In July 2026 Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reported that the launch had moved from late 2026 to an unveiling at WWDC in June 2027 and a release late that year, with Apple spending the extra time on engineering and on how it explains privacy. The reported design has two cameras (one for photos and video, one for computer vision), speakers and microphones but no display, which puts it up against Ray-Ban Meta rather than display glasses. Until Apple announces it, treat every detail as a report, not a specification.
