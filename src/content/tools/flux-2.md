FLUX 3 is Black Forest Labs' multimodal model. The image endpoint launched on 1 October 2026 and places each element on a 0–1000 grid by bounding box, edits one element without touching the rest, takes up to 10 reference images and renders up to about 5,456 x 3,072 pixels (per Morphic's model page). The main model is API-only, with a commercial weights licence for self-hosting. On 15 October 2026 the company published FLUX 3 Image Klein, a 5.6B-parameter open-weights model, and says it leads the company's own blind evaluations for text-to-image. That gives builders a downloadable FLUX 3 option, so FLUX.2 is no longer the only one.
