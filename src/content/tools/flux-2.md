FLUX 3 is Black Forest Labs' new multimodal model: its video side shipped first and ranks sixth on Artificial Analysis's text-to-video leaderboard, and the image endpoint followed on 1 October 2026. The image model places each element on a 0–1000 grid by bounding box, edits one element without touching the rest, takes up to 10 reference images and renders up to about 5,456 x 3,072 pixels. It is not open at launch: Black Forest Labs sells a commercial weights licence for self-hosting and says an open-weights version will follow "in the following weeks". Until then FLUX.2 remains the family's downloadable option, though the best FLUX.2 variant now sits only around 20th to 30th on Artificial Analysis's image leaderboard.
