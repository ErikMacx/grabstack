Personal Computer is the desktop side of Perplexity Computer: it reads and writes folders you connect, drives Mail, Finder, Slack and other native apps, and on a Mac mini keeps working in the background while you send it tasks from your iPhone. The June entry was wrong on price: the Mac version needs a Pro or Max plan, not Max alone; Windows followed on 28 July, initially for Max and Enterprise Max, with approval prompts before sensitive actions and an activity log. Since then Hybrid Compute on Mac (1 September) splits a task between cloud models and a local one (Gemma 4 E4B, Qwen3.6 35B-A3B or Perplexity's own), with an on-device privacy gate that can mask or hold back sensitive content, and Portable Computer runs local-first on NVIDIA hardware without spending credits. It is the most polished commercial take on the always-on desktop agent, and a working example of the local-plus-cloud split others are now copying.
