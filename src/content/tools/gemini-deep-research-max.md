Deep Research Max is the most thorough tier of Google's Deep Research agent, built on Gemini 3.1 Pro and offered to developers through the Gemini API's Interactions API, where it must run in the background and be polled for results. It supports remote MCP servers, File Search and native charts, and is still a public preview under the deep-research-max-preview-04-2026 ID. No material change found since June 2026.
