Workspace agents are the Codex-powered shared agents OpenAI introduced on 22 April for Business, Enterprise and Edu plans. OpenAI's help pages could not be opened in this check, so their current preview or general-availability status is not confirmed here. The bigger change is ChatGPT Work, launched on 9 July: a separate surface that takes a brief, works for hours across your connected apps and a built-in browser, and returns documents, spreadsheets, decks or web apps, metered like Codex rather than chat. It arrived with a desktop app that puts Chat, Work and Codex side by side and a Plugin Directory in place of the old app directory. OpenAI's "What's new" page, which dates entries by week, shows GPT-6.1 Sol in the week of 28 September, an Ultrafast Sol option for Pro and eligible Enterprise and Edu plans in the week of 5 October, and GPT-6.1 Terra, aimed at visual reasoning and computer use, in the week of 26 October. OpenAI's always-on dots, launched the same week as Work, are covered separately.
