Workspace agents, the Codex-powered shared agents OpenAI previewed on 22 April for Business, Enterprise, Edu and Teachers plans, are still labelled a research preview, with credit-based pricing after an initial free period. The bigger change is ChatGPT Work, launched on 9 July: a separate surface that takes a brief, works for hours across your connected apps and a built-in browser, and returns documents, spreadsheets, decks or web apps, metered like Codex rather than chat. It arrived with a desktop app that puts Chat, Work and Codex side by side and a Plugin Directory in place of the old app directory, and it moved to GPT-6 Sol in September and GPT-6.1 Sol on 29 September. OpenAI's always-on dots, launched the same week, are covered separately.
