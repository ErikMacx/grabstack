BrowserOS is a free, open-source browser (AGPL-3.0) for macOS, Windows and Linux. Its site says you bring your own AI provider keys, and it lists an Enterprise option with no pricing. Agents such as Claude Code or Cursor connect over MCP and work in isolated tabs, using accounts you are already logged in to. Simular Pro, announced on 8 July 2025, pairs a neural agent that explores a task with a symbolic agent that runs it as repeatable code. It needs macOS 15 or later. We found no pricing on Simular's announcement page. The BrowserOS site (checked 2 November 2026) names its current product BrowserOS neo, formerly BrowserClaw, and does not mention Simular.
