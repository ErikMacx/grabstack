Grok 4.7 launched on 21 September 2026 as the company's frontier model for coding, agents and knowledge work, after Grok 4.5 (8 July) and Grok 4.6 (12 August), all at the same $2/$6 per million tokens. It has a 500K-token context, doubles in price above 200K prompt tokens, and adds an xhigh reasoning-effort setting; a faster Grok 4.7 Fast variant is only available in Cursor and Grok Build. Since June xAI has rebranded as SpaceXAI following its merger with SpaceX, though its developer docs still use both names. SpaceXAI publishes no benchmark figures in its release notes, so compare it on your own tasks.
