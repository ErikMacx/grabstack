SpaceX acquired xAI in an all-stock deal on 2 February 2026, and Grok is now presented as a SpaceXAI product. The Grok product page no longer uses the DeepSearch name: it sells search as "Live X integration" for breaking news and posts plus "real-time web search", with multi-agent reasoning on the paid SuperGrok tier. Grok 4.5 reached the apps in July, and xAI's attention has since shifted to agents (Grok Bot, August) and coding models (Grok 4.7, September, $2/$6 per million tokens on the API). Its access to X remains the one thing rivals cannot copy, so it is still the pick for fast-moving news and sentiment.
