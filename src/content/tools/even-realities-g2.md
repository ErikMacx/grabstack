Even G2 is a pair of 35 g glasses with a green micro-LED display (1,200 nits) and no camera, built for productivity: live translation, a teleprompter, navigation, meeting transcription with on-screen context cues, and the "Hey, Even" assistant. The optional R1 smart ring ($249) controls the glasses and tracks health. In July 2026 Even Realities raised a $150M round led by Meituan and Tencent at a $1B valuation. The maker claims up to two days of battery; TechCrunch did not test that. With no camera, it is the clearest alternative for people who want a heads-up display without the privacy fights that camera glasses bring.
