Meta agreed to buy Manus for about $2B in December 2025, but China's National Development and Reform Commission blocked the deal on 27 April 2026, Meta cut Manus off from its systems in June, and Manus resumed independent operations under its founding team in early September after backing up and restoring user data. It then shipped Manus 2.0 on 28 September, built on a new in-house agent architecture called Cascade, with a rebuilt Studio workspace, an always-on Cloud Computer you can buy, and Cue, a personal-agent app in invite-only early access; Manus Flex followed a day later, letting you use your own API key. For anyone building on it, the product is moving fast but the ownership saga is a reminder that cross-border politics can reach a tool you depend on.
