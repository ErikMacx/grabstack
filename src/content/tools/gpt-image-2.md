ChatGPT Images 2.5 launched on 8 September 2026 for all ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work and Codex users, and is in the API as gpt-image-2.5-flare and gpt-image-2.5-sunburst. OpenAI says it is up to 50% faster than Images 2.0 and keeps subjects and details consistent across several rounds of edits. It also adds a Sketch tool, templates and comment-based edits in ChatGPT, plus new xhigh and max quality levels and custom sizes in the API, at the same token prices as gpt-image-2. If you still call gpt-image-1.5 or gpt-image-1-mini, OpenAI shuts both down on 1 December 2026; DALL-E 2 and 3 were already retired on 12 May 2026.
