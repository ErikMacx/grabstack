GPT Image 2.6 reached the API on 4 November 2026 as gpt-image-2.6-flare and gpt-image-2.6-sunburst, according to OpenAI's changelog. It adds a complexity control and SVG and animated GIF output, and markup-guided editing works with both 2.5 and 2.6. OpenAI's docs list Sunburst at $30 per million image output tokens. Images 2.5 (8 September 2026) remains available and still ranks second and fifth on the Artificial Analysis leaderboard. OpenAI's deprecations page says gpt-image-1.5 and gpt-image-1-mini shut down on 1 December 2026, and it recommends the 2.6 models instead.
