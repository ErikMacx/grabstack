Dots are OpenAI's answer to OpenClaw-style personal agents: you give one an ongoing responsibility, and it works on its own cloud computer between conversations, learns from your feedback and can be reached from ChatGPT on desktop, web and mobile, Slack or Teams. The first dot comes at no extra cost on Pro and Business Premium, with usage not counted against plan limits for the first month; extra dots and more capacity are promised later at undisclosed prices, and Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare admins can switch on a beta. At launch the Pro rollout excluded the UK, the EEA and Switzerland. It is the first time a frontier lab has shipped an always-on agent as a mainstream subscription feature, which puts direct pressure on self-hosted and desktop rivals.
