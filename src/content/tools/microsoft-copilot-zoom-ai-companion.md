The meeting platforms keep absorbing the notetaker market. Zoom launched ZoomMate on 1 June 2026, an AI work surface that turns meeting context into finished tasks, from $20 per user a month with AI credits in North America; its AI Companion notetaker also now works in Microsoft Teams and Google Meet. Microsoft added a Teams policy (rolled out August–September 2026) that detects third-party recording and notetaking bots and holds them in the lobby for organiser approval by default. Its Facilitator agent is gaining proactive web answers in meetings for Microsoft 365 Copilot licence holders, now scheduled for general availability between mid-November and mid-December 2026.
