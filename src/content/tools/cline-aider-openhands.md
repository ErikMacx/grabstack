Cline is an Apache 2.0 agent for VS Code, JetBrains, Cursor and the terminal, and in September 2026 added its own desktop app aimed at open-weight models; it says more than 11 million developers use it and it works with every major provider or with local models through Ollama. OpenHands, under an MIT core licence, now offers a local CLI, a hosted cloud with an "Agent Canvas" and an SDK for building your own agents, and works with Claude, Codex and Gemini as well as open-weight models such as Kimi, DeepSeek and Qwen. Aider remains a well-liked terminal pair programmer, but its published release history still ends at v0.86.1, with newer model support only on its main branch. All three are free to run with your own API keys, which keeps model choice and cost in your hands.
