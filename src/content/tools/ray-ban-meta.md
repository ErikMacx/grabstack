At Connect in September 2026 Meta released Ray-Ban Meta (Gen 3): slimmer frames, up to nine hours of battery, the same 12 MP camera with 3K video, and a six-microphone array. It also announced Ray-Ban Meta Audio, its first AI glasses without a camera (43 g, up to 12 hours, $349, shipping 13 October), a $249 Meta Adventurer frame, and said its Muse AI agent is coming to the glasses in the coming months. The Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses are expanding to the UK, Canada, France, Italy and Germany (£749 in the UK). A camera-free model is Meta's answer to the privacy backlash, and the Muse agent is the change to watch if you build for the glasses.
