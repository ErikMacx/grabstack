Operator was OpenAI's browser-driving research preview, launched in January 2025 on its Computer-Using Agent model, which scored 38.1% on OSWorld at the time. In July 2025 OpenAI merged it into ChatGPT agent and later closed the separate Operator site. On 9 July 2026 OpenAI launched ChatGPT Work for longer, multi-step tasks, which can use a built-in browser and, on desktop, local files and apps with your permission; OpenAI's help pages now say ChatGPT agent is no longer available, and the Atlas browser was scheduled to stop working on 9 August 2026. If you want OpenAI's computer-use agent today, look at ChatGPT Work.
