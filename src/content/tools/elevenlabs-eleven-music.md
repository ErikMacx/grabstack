Eleven v4 and v4 Turbo are a new text-to-speech architecture: cloning from 10 seconds of audio, 90 languages (up from 70 in v3), stackable inline tags for expression, and a Turbo mode that starts speaking while an LLM is still writing its reply, which cuts voice-agent latency. Music v2.5 (11 September) brings more realistic instruments and better song structure, was preferred most of the time across 47,885 blind comparison pairs, and you own the tracks you make, though downloads are blocked on tracks that reference other artists' songs. On 14 September the official ElevenLabs MCP connector gained speech, transcription, dubbing, music, sound effects, image and video generation, with OAuth sign-in for Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor. TechCrunch reports annualised revenue above $600M, ElevenLabs says a $300M employee tender on 30 September, led by Wellington and T. Rowe Price, values it at $22B, double the $11B of its February 2026 $500M raise. That was a share sale by staff, not new capital. No Eleven Music change was found since 11 September.
