Eleven v4 and v4 Turbo are a new text-to-speech architecture: cloning from 10 seconds of audio, 90 languages (up from 70 in v3), stackable inline tags for expression, and a Turbo mode that starts speaking while an LLM is still writing its reply, which cuts voice-agent latency. Music v2.5 (11 September) brings more realistic instruments and better song structure, was preferred most of the time across 47,885 blind comparison pairs, and you own the tracks you make, though downloads are blocked on tracks that reference other artists' songs. On 14 September the official ElevenLabs MCP connector gained speech, transcription, dubbing, music, sound effects, image and video generation, with OAuth sign-in for Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor. TechCrunch reports annualised revenue above $600M, after a $500M raise at an $11B valuation in February 2026.
