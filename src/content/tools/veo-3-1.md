Veo 3.1 is no longer Google's lead video model. The Gemini API docs now say "Use Gemini Omni Flash as your default model for video generation" and keep Veo 3.1 for specific features or existing pipelines, and Google shut down the older Veo 2.0 and 3.0 models on 30 June 2026. On Artificial Analysis, Veo 3.1 ranks 11th for image-to-video and 18th for text-to-video, behind Omni Flash, so its June position as category leader no longer holds. Keep using it if your pipeline depends on it, but test Gemini Omni for new work.
