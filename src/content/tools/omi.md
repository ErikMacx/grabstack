Omi is a small pendant (worn on a cord, clip or wristband) that records conversations in 25+ languages, online or offline, and turns them into notes and memories in its apps for iPhone, Android, Mac and the web. Its software is open source, so you can inspect the code, run it locally and build your own apps for its app store, which the maker says has over 1,000 integrations. An Omi Glass developer kit adds a camera. No material change found since June 2026.
