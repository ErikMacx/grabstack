GPT-6.2 Astra is OpenAI's top model as of 17 November 2026, replacing GPT-6 Astra, which arrived in September as the first GPT-6 model. It costs $8/$40 per million tokens, 20% less than GPT-6 Astra, and is in ChatGPT and Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users and in the API as gpt-6.2-astra. OpenAI's own tables claim gains over GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.2 on agentic and professional-work benchmarks; these are the maker's figures. Its companion GPT-6.2 Sol costs $2/$10, and for most API work the cheaper tier matters more.
