Antigravity 2.0, launched at Google I/O on 19 May 2026, is a desktop command centre for running several coding agents in parallel, paired with a Go-based Antigravity CLI that replaced Gemini CLI, which stopped serving free, Google AI Pro and Ultra users on 18 June 2026. Since June Google has shipped releases almost weekly, adding custom agents, IDE extensions, remote control, plugin management, a plan-before-you-build mode and, in v2.19.1, direct messages to sub-agents. Each new Gemini Flash model has followed it in, from 3.5 Flash at launch through 3.6 and 3.7 to Gemini 3.8 Flash on 1 September ($0.75/$3.75 per million tokens on the API). Individuals use it free with weekly limits, Google AI Pro and Ultra raise those limits, and businesses pay through Google Cloud from $30 a seat or pay as they go.
