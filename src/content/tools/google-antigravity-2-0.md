Antigravity 2.0, launched at Google I/O on 19 May 2026, is a desktop command centre for running several coding agents in parallel, paired with a Go-based Antigravity CLI that replaced Gemini CLI, which stopped serving free, Google AI Pro and Ultra users on 18 June 2026. Releases come almost weekly. Gemini 4 Neon reached Ultra, Pro and Enterprise on 18 November and became the default model, and v2.34.0 (20 November) added it for eligible paid consumer plans, with MCP Apps support and a redesigned diff view. v2.35.2 (25 November) lets you switch models while a turn is running. The free individual tier has a weekly quota, and Google says some of its models will be removed on 30 November 2026. Google AI Pro and Ultra raise the limits, and Pro lists Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 for non-trial subscriptions. Businesses pay through Google Cloud from $30 a seat a month, or pay as they go.
