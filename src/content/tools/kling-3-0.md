Kling 3.0 (February 2026) has been overtaken on Artificial Analysis by Seedance, MiniMax H3, Gemini Omni Flash and Wan 3.0, and ranks around 15th to 23rd across its text-to-video and image-to-video boards. Kuaishou announced Kling 4.0 on 28 September 2026, with clips of up to 30 seconds (double 3.0's 15), up to 10 keyframes, mixed image, video and voice references, and output up to 4K with stereo audio. So far only the lighter Kling 4.0 Flash is live, for Ultra Yearly subscribers; the full model and API are targeted for October but not guaranteed, so do not plan around it yet.
