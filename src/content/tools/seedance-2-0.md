Seedance 2.5 launched in China on 31 July 2026 and reached developers outside China through BytePlus ModelArk on 6 August, though BytePlus is not available in the United States. The headline changes are length and editing: clips of up to 30 seconds, up to 50 image, video, audio and text references, and follow-up edits to specific timestamps or characters. It is also on Runway, which added a cheaper 480p draft mode on 2 October, and on Pika and other hosts. It is no longer alone at the top: Wan 3.0 and MiniMax H3-based models sit level with it within Artificial Analysis's margins of error.
