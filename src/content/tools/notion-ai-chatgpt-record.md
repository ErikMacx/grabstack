Both Notion and ChatGPT entered the meeting-notes space in 2025, competing with standalone notetakers from inside tools people already pay for. On 29 September 2026 OpenAI released a Meetings plugin in beta for the ChatGPT macOS desktop app, for Pro and Business users with Enterprise to follow; it writes personalised summaries with action items into ChatGPT Space, and the audio is deleted once the notes are ready. Notion's meeting notes could not be re-checked against Notion's own pages for this update.
