Meta acquired Limitless on 5 December 2025. The Pendant stopped being sold to new customers, the Rewind Mac app's capture was switched off on 19 December 2025, and the service was withdrawn in the UK, the EU and several other countries. Existing Pendant owners elsewhere were promised support for at least a year, with the Unlimited plan made free. Amazon bought Bee in July 2025, showed it at CES 2026 as a clip-on or wrist-worn companion, and says it will stay separate from Alexa+ for now while it adds features. No material change found since June 2026.
