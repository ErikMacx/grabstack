HeyGen's flagship is now Avatar V, with lip-sync in more than 175 languages and a Video Agent that turns a prompt into a finished video. On 30 September 2026 it also launched HeyGen Video (heygen-video-1), its first general text-to-video model, post-trained on MiniMax H3, making 5–15 second clips at up to 768p with audio for a promotional $0.01 a second through October; the standard rate afterwards is unclear. Synthesia has moved towards interactive avatars, with Roleplay and Survey Sessions, an Interactive Avatars API and an MCP server. Google's Gemini 3.8 Live added a lip-synced Live Avatar for enterprise customers on 24 September, so avatar video is becoming a feature of the big model platforms too.
