Exa runs its own index and returns token-efficient results and extracted content that agents can use directly, with search across the web, news, companies, research papers, people and financial data, and APIs for agentic research and monitoring. In May 2026 it raised a $250M Series C led by Andreessen Horowitz, with Nvidia and Y Combinator, at a $1.95B valuation. Cognition says Exa powers search in Devin, and the company claims more than 500,000 developers. If you are building an agent that needs the live web, this is one of the main contested layers, with Perplexity and Brave among the search APIs Exa benchmarks itself against.
