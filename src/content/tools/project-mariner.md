Project Mariner was a Google research prototype that drove a Chrome browser to complete multi-step web tasks. Google Labs records it as discontinued on 4 May 2026. We could not find an official explanation or a named successor, so the earlier claims about why it closed and where its technology went have been removed until they can be sourced.
