Devin is Cognition's autonomous coding agent: it works in cloud virtual machines, takes tasks from Slack, Microsoft Teams or the terminal, and splits work across parallel cloud agents. Since June Cognition has put its products on one Devin plan (Free, Pro $20, Max $200, Teams $80 plus $40 a seat) covering Devin Cloud, Devin Desktop (formerly Windsurf), the CLI and the API, and in September it let you hand a task from the terminal to a cloud session and back. Devin now offers models from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and SpaceXAI plus Cognition's own SWE-2, and on 28 September Cognition said it had become 30–40% cheaper per task in its standard modes. In September Cognition raised $2 billion at a $48 billion valuation, reporting $900 million in annualised revenue, up from $492 million in May.
