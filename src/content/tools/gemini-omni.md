Gemini Omni, unveiled at Google I/O in May 2026, is a model family that Google says "can create anything from any input — starting with video": you mix text, images, audio and video as input, get video with sound, and then edit it by talking to it. Omni Flash is in the Gemini app and Google Flow for Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers, and is free inside YouTube Shorts. Omni 1.1 Flash (27 August) added scene extension in 10-second steps up to 40 seconds, first-and-last-frame control, a 360p draft mode at a third of the cost, and 1080p or 4K upscaling, and is generally available in the Gemini API as gemini-omni-1.1-flash. Google's own docs now name it the default video model ahead of Veo 3.1, and it ranks third for image-to-video on Artificial Analysis, within the margin of error of the leaders. On 5 November Google released Omni 1.2 Flash, which makes native clips of 3 to 15 seconds, cuts the price 30% to $0.07 per second at 720p, and is generally available in the Gemini API and Google Cloud. Google's pricing page says requests to the 1.1 model route to 1.2 from 3 December 2026. Google published no benchmark scores for 1.2, so the ranking above is for 1.1.
