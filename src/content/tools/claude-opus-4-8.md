Claude Opus 5.5, released on 22 September 2026, is the first model in Anthropic's Claude 5.5 family and the one Anthropic now tells developers to start with for most workloads. Since June the Opus line has moved from 4.8 through Opus 5, and Anthropic now also sells a pricier top tier, Claude Fable 5.1 ($10/$50 per million tokens), plus a Mythos 5.1 variant with looser safeguards that is limited to vetted cyber-defence and life-sciences organisations. Opus 5.5 costs $4/$20 per million tokens with $0.20 cache reads, has a 1M-token context, and on Anthropic's own tests beats Fable 5.1 on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (66.4% against 55.8%). For builders this means near-top-tier coding and agent performance at well under half Fable's price; Claude Sonnet 5.5 ($2/$10) is also now available.
