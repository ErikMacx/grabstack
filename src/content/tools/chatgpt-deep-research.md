Deep research is ChatGPT's agent for cited, structured reports: OpenAI says a task can take 5 to 30 minutes, it can be limited to sites you choose and to connected apps, and reports export to Markdown, Word or PDF. OpenAI's own update log for it stops at 10 February 2026, when app connections and site filtering arrived. No material change found since June 2026. Note that ChatGPT Work, launched in July, now overlaps with it for research that ends in a finished document.
