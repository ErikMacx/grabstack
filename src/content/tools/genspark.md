Genspark sells an all-in-one AI workspace built around agents that carry out tasks for you. On 21 July it launched AI Workspace 6.0: SecondBrain (a memory layer across email, meetings and documents), SecondBrain Note (a card-sized recording device, $199 or $179 at the introductory price), GenMail, GenTeam, AI Design and AgentBase. In June it had extended its Series B to $485M at a $2.6B post-money valuation (company press release). The 6.0 details come from the company's own release as reposted by Webull. Later product changes could not be confirmed; the company's press page blocked our fetch.
