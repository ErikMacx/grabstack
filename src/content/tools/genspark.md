Genspark sells an all-in-one AI workspace built around agents that carry out tasks for you. On 17 June it added $100M to its Series B, taking it to $485M at a $2.6B post-money valuation, up from $1.6B in March, hired monday.com's former North America sales chief as chief revenue officer, and introduced AgentBase, which builds CRMs, HR systems and project trackers as AI-native databases and dashboards. That points it squarely at replacing small-business SaaS rather than just answering questions. No later product changes could be confirmed for this update.
