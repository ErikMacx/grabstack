MiniMax H3, the third-generation Hailuo model, launched on 31 July 2026 and produces up to 15 seconds of 2K video with native stereo audio from mixed text, image, video and audio inputs. Its weights followed on 3 August under a community licence that excludes the EU, the UK, South Korea and the US and needs separate permission above $20M a year in revenue, so "open" does not mean usable everywhere. Luma's Ray3.2 (9 June 2026) adds frame-level control with up to 16 keyframes, performance tracking for up to eight faces, native HDR and 16-bit EXR export, with an API. Pika has become a hub that offers Seedance 2.5, MiniMax H3, Wan 3.0 and Gemini Omni Flash alongside its own Pika 2.5, and neither Luma's nor Pika's own models appear near the top of Artificial Analysis's video rankings.
