Claude Fable 5.2 was released on 9 November 2026 and is available on all platforms, including AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure, as `claude-fable-5-2`. Anthropic says it is the same model as Mythos 5.2, which has looser cyber and biology safeguards and is limited to vetted organisations. On Anthropic's own tests it beats Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5 on most listed benchmarks, though Opus 5.5 scores higher on one Elo comparison. For builders it is the costlier option next to Opus 5.5, with a fast mode and the ability to act as an advisor or orchestrator.
