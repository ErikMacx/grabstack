MAI-Thinking-1 is Microsoft AI's first reasoning model. It is a sparse mixture-of-experts transformer with 35B active and about 1T total parameters, a 256K-token context window and up to 64K output tokens. It takes text only and is in preview on Azure and Microsoft Foundry. Microsoft says it was trained without distillation from third-party models. The benchmark scores are Microsoft's own and were not independently checked.
