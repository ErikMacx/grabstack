Google announced Gemini 4 Argon on 30 September 2026, but it is rolling out only to trusted cyber defenders through Google's Fairwind Program; paid API customers and Google AI Ultra subscribers are next, with no date given. Argon raises the output limit from 64K to 1M tokens and will cost $2/$10 per million tokens at first, rising to $4/$20 later. Until it opens up, Gemini 3.1 Pro is still only a preview, and Google's strongest generally available model is Gemini 3.8 Flash (2 September), which Google calls its best reasoning and coding model yet, at an introductory $0.75/$3.75 per million tokens until 31 December 2026. Builders should plan on 3.8 Flash today and treat Argon's benchmark claims as Google's own until people can test it.
