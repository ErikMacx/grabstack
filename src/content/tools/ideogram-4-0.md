Ideogram 4.5 replaced 4.0 as the current model on 30 September 2026, aimed at multi-turn editing: Ideogram says it reduces drift and keeps detail across repeated edits, and edited crops stitch back into the original cleanly. It comes in four speeds (Turbo, Balanced, Quality, High) through Ideogram's app, its API and partners, and Runway added it to Runway MCP on 2 October. On Artificial Analysis it scores almost the same as 4.0 (Elo 1,013 against 1,011) and sits around 34th for text-to-image, partly because no public benchmark measures quality after many edits. Ideogram has said open weights for 4.5 are planned, but none had been published at launch.
