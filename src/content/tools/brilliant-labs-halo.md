Halo is a pair of lightweight AI glasses with a small colour display, a low-power camera, microphones and bone-conduction speakers, running Brilliant Labs' Noa assistant with long-term memory. It was announced in mid-2025 for late-2025 shipping, but mass production only began in July 2026, with the maker scheduling first shipments for early August 2026 and raising the price from the $349 pre-order price to $399. The hardware designs and code are open source, and you can build your own mini-apps by describing them in plain language. That makes it the most hackable display glasses for people who want to build on the device rather than just use it.
