Firefly is now both Adobe's own model family and a single app for many makers' models. Adobe's page says "Adobe Firefly models are commercially safe" and trained on licensed Adobe Stock and public-domain content, but it does not extend that promise to the partner models it also offers, so check which model made an image before you rely on it commercially. Firefly Image 5 is still Adobe's newest image model, and Custom Models, which trains Firefly on your own images to match a brand or style, is in public beta. No new Firefly image model has appeared since June 2026; Adobe MAX runs from 10 to 12 November 2026.
