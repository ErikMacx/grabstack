Fathom is an AI meeting notetaker known for its generous free plan, which still offers unlimited recordings and transcripts, instant AI summaries and a choice of bot or bot-free capture. On 14 September 2026 Superhuman, the company formerly called Grammarly, bought it for an undisclosed sum and plans to tie meeting notes into its email, calendar, docs and agent tools. Fathom had about 400,000 monthly active users. Expect the free plan and standalone product to change as the integration proceeds, so check the terms before building a workflow on it.
