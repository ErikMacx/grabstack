Recraft's V4.1 family, released in May 2026, is still current, and Artificial Analysis now also lists a V4.1 Flash variant dated September 2026. The June claim that it was the highest-ranked text-to-image model outside Google and OpenAI no longer holds: xAI's Grok Imagine Image 2.0, Microsoft's MAI-Image models, Alibaba's Qwen-Image 3.0 and ByteDance's Seedream 5.0 Pro all rank above it. On raw image quality it is now a mid-table option, so compare it against the newer leaders before choosing it.
