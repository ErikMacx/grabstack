Fireflies joins calls as a bot, transcribes in more than 100 languages and offers summaries, search and its AskFred assistant, with a free tier and paid plans from $10 per seat a month on annual billing. On 29 September 2026 it launched Fireflies Talk, free unlimited dictation into any app on Mac and Windows, on every plan, which undercuts paid dictation apps. Bot-based notetakers face more friction in Microsoft Teams, which since August 2026 holds detected recording bots in the lobby for the organiser to approve by default.
