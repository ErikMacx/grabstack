Fireflies joins calls as a bot, supports English, Spanish, French, Portuguese and Italian, with 100+ more languages listed as beta and offers summaries, search and its AskFred assistant, with a free tier and paid Pro, Business and Enterprise plans charged per seat (its pricing page shows no amounts, so check it). On 29 September 2026 it launched Fireflies Talk, free unlimited dictation into any app on Mac and Windows, on every plan, which undercuts paid dictation apps. Bot-based notetakers face more friction in Microsoft Teams, which since August 2026 holds detected recording bots in the lobby for the organiser to approve by default.
