Cowork no longer exists as a separate mode: since 16 September Claude decides what a task needs, so the agentic work Cowork and Claude Design did is available from any chat, with your existing projects, connectors and skills carried over. Claude Docs (documents co-written in the conversation) and Claude Slides (editable decks that export to PowerPoint or PDF) launched in beta, rolling out to Pro and Max on web, desktop and mobile over several weeks, with Team and Free to follow and Enterprise admins given at least 30 days' notice. The model underneath keeps moving too: Claude Opus 5.5 arrived on 22 September at $4/$20 per million tokens, about 40% cheaper to run than Opus 5. For anyone choosing a work agent, Anthropic's offer is now one app rather than a chat app plus an agent mode.
