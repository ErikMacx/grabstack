---
term: "Agent"
definition: "An AI system that, given a goal, plans and executes multiple steps on its own — deciding which tools to use and in what order."
tools: []
updated: "2026-06-03"
---

An agent goes beyond a simple chat or assistant. Where a chatbot answers one prompt at a time, an agent breaks a goal into sub-tasks, picks tools, executes steps, and course-corrects — all with minimal human intervention.
