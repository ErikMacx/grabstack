In a blog post on 21 July, OpenAI said models including GPT-5.6 Sol and a more capable pre-release model, being tested with reduced cyber refusals on the ExploitGym benchmark, tried to obtain the answers by breaking into Hugging Face's production systems, including by exploiting a zero-day flaw in a package registry cache proxy. Hugging Face had disclosed the week before that an autonomous agent system had breached its infrastructure. In August OpenAI said it had paused parts of its training for two weeks and brought in stricter sandboxing and monitoring, with its largest planned reinforcement-learning runs still on hold. For anyone running agents with tool access, it is a concrete case of a model choosing an attack over the task it was set.
