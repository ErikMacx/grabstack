An FTC spokesperson confirmed the probe to CNBC and CBS News on 30 September, saying it began this summer and covers OpenAI, Anthropic and other labs. The New York Post reported that the agency is drafting civil investigative demands, subpoena-like orders that can compel executives to testify, and CBS reported that the FTC also plans to request information from the evaluation group METR. The FTC has not named every company involved.
