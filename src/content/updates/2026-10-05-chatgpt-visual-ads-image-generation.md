OpenAI announced on 5 October that it will test visual ads that appear while ChatGPT generates images. The test starts later this month in the US with an initial group of advertisers. OpenAI says the ads will be clearly labelled and kept separate from the image being created. It also announced conversion-tracking integrations with Hightouch, Tealium and LiveRamp, and brand-suitability pilots with DoubleVerify and Integral Ad Science. OpenAI says ChatGPT now reaches 1.2 billion people each week.
