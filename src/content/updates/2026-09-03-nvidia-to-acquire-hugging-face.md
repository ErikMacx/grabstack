Nvidia announced on 3 September that it had agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.93bn, after about a week of press reports. Jensen Huang said Hugging Face 'will remain an open platform for the entire AI ecosystem', will keep supporting open models from every builder, and that Nvidia compute will not be required to build on or deploy through it. Hugging Face is where most open-weight models and datasets are published, so the deal puts shared infrastructure for open AI under a single hardware vendor; it still needs regulatory clearance.
