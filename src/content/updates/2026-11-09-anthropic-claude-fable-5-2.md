Anthropic says Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 are one underlying model. Fable has cyber and biology safeguards; Mythos is limited to organisations in its Cyber and Life Sciences Verification Programs. Fable 5.2 costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, with a fast mode at $16/$80. On Anthropic's own tests it scores 49.1% on FrontierCode 2.0 against 45.5% for Opus 5.5 and 32.4% for Fable 5.1. Anthropic also says outputs are up to 30% faster than Fable 5.1.
