Anthropic priced its IPO at $83.00 a share on 18 November, above its $70–$78 range, and shares began trading on Nasdaq as "ANT" on 19 November. The offering closed on 20 November at 1,127,000,000 Class A shares, after the underwriters fully exercised their option for 147,000,000 more. Of the total, Anthropic sold 1,103,351,354 shares and existing stockholders sold 23,648,646. In a concurrent private placement, it sold 313,253,007 Class N shares at $83.00, about $26.0 billion, to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA and TSMC Partners.
