Grok 4.7, released on 21 September under the SpaceXAI name, is described in its launch post as the company's most capable model for coding and knowledge work, working longer on hard tasks and checking its own work more carefully. It is available in Cursor and Grok Build, through the Grok API and via third-party coding harnesses, routers and cloud platforms, with a faster variant at double the price. Early independent reviews called it a modest step up on Grok 4.6.
