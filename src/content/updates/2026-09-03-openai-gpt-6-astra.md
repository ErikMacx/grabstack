OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra on 3 September, first to enterprise customers in its Daybreak programme and then, over the following days, to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise users and through the API and AWS. OpenAI calls it state of the art on computer use, browsing, software engineering, cyber-security, science and professional work, and priced it at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens. OpenAI says Astra meets the Critical threshold for cyber-security under its Preparedness Framework. It now powers OpenAI's Dots agents and sets the ceiling for the cheaper GPT-6 Sol and Luna models that followed.
