Anthropic launched its roadshow for its proposed initial public offering. It expects a price of $70.00 to $78.00 a share for 825,000,000 Class A shares: 801,351,354 from the company and 23,648,646 from existing stockholders. Underwriters have an option for up to 123,750,000 more shares. The listing is applied for on the Nasdaq Global Select Market as "ANT". No pricing or trading date is given.
