Anthropic announced on 14 October that it has opened an office in Singapore and signed a Memorandum of Understanding with the country's Ministry of Digital Development and Information. The MOU covers AI safety, security and model evaluation, research on AI adoption, and responsible use in the public and private sectors. Anthropic says GovTech Singapore, Grab and DBS already use Claude, and that Singapore ranks second of 121 countries for Claude.ai use relative to population in its own Economic Index.
