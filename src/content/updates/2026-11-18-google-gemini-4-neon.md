Google says Gemini 4 Neon sits between Carbon, for the hardest long-horizon projects, and Hydrogen, for fast everyday work. It has a 1 million token context window and costs $3 input and $15 output per million tokens. Google says it costs 32% less per task than Carbon at the default thinking level. Google's blog gives its own benchmark claims, including a score of 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, so treat them as the maker's own.
