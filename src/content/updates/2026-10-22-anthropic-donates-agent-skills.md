Anthropic said on 22 October that it is donating Agent Skills, its open standard for giving AI agents specialist knowledge and workflows, to the Agentic AI Foundation. The foundation already hosts the Model Context Protocol. Anthropic says more than 70 agent platforms have adopted the standard since December 2025, and that the ecosystem has about 1 million published skills. These figures are Anthropic's own. It says the standard will stay vendor-neutral, with maintainers from several companies.
