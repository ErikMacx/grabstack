Google says Gemini 4 Carbon is now available through Google AI Studio and Gemini for business for paying developers and enterprises. Google AI Ultra subscribers get it in Antigravity, the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search. The price is $2/$10 per million tokens until 30 November, then $4/$20. Google says Argon remains the better choice for most software work. The benchmark and safety figures are Google's own.
