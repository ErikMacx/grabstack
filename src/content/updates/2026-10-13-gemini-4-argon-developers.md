Google said on 13 October that developers can build with Gemini 4 Argon. It is available on the paid tier of the Gemini API in Google AI Studio and in Android Studio. Google AI Ultra subscribers can try it in AI Studio and in Google Antigravity. Google's post gives no pricing or benchmark figures, so the launch-day price and benchmark claims from 30 September still stand as Google's own.
