Mistral announced Large 4 on 6 October. It has 1 trillion parameters in total, 49 billion of them active, takes text and image input, and combines instruct and reasoning modes. It is in public preview through Mistral Studio at $1.36 per million input tokens and $4.18 per million output tokens, and Mistral says the open weights will follow by the end of the month. The benchmark figures in the announcement are Mistral's own and have not been independently checked.
