Anthropic published its 2026 Usage Policy update on 8 October, and it takes effect on 12 November 2026. Rules on fake accounts and influence operations move into one section. The elections section is narrowed to deceiving voters or disrupting elections. Claude cannot be used to decide who to investigate, arrest or charge, and Claude connected to hardware that acts on its own needs a way to stop the equipment and a safe state if disconnected. The update also adds a prohibition on sustained, pointless cruelty toward Claude.
