Google's launch post says Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash generates and edits video natively for 3 to 15 seconds, up from 10 seconds with Omni 1.1. It is generally available through the Gemini API and Google Cloud, and Google cut the price by 30% to $0.07 per second at 720p. The post gives no benchmark scores, only example comparisons. Google's pricing page says requests to gemini-omni-1.1-flash will route to 1.2 from 3 December 2026.
