Anthropic said on 15 October that it has started giving six employees of METR, a nonprofit that evaluates frontier AI models, company hardware, office space and internal tool access. Anthropic cannot veto METR's publications, but it can require redaction of narrow categories such as customer data, privileged legal information and model architecture. It also named SecureBio, which is reviewing the chemical and biological weapons chapter of its August 2026 Risk Report, and Accenture and Faculty as partners. This is Anthropic's own description of the arrangement.
