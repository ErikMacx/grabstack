On 29 September, after a lunch with President Trump, Sundar Pichai, Dario Amodei, Mark Zuckerberg, Greg Brockman, Jensen Huang and Elon Musk signed the 'White House Accord on Super Intelligence: Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities'. The companies commit to taking responsibility for the safety of their own systems, adding safeguards, detecting and fixing problems quickly, and working with independent auditors. The one-page document sets no consequences for breaches, and Trump called it 'morally binding'. It confirms the administration's preference for voluntary commitments over formal regulation.
