Beijing-based Moonshot AI launched Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with a 1-million-token context window and native image input, through its API, its apps and OpenRouter at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens. Artificial Analysis placed it fourth of 189 models on its Intelligence Index at launch, behind Claude Fable 5 and two GPT-5.6 Sol configurations. The full weights followed on 27 July under a bespoke Kimi K3 licence with commercial restrictions, rather than the Modified MIT licence used for K2. It put an open-weight Chinese model within reach of the US frontier sooner than many observers had expected.
