Mistral released Voxtral 3: Small and Mini Transcribe 3 (50+ languages), a realtime transcription model with sub-500ms latency, and Mini TTS 3 (35+ languages, 50+ dialects). Listed prices start at $0.0035 a minute for batch transcription, and TTS is $13 per million characters. The models run on Mistral's EU-hosted API, and self-deployment is offered. Mistral's page gives no licence.
