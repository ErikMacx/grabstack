Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5 on 7 October, following Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5. It is the company's fastest and cheapest model, aimed at high-volume work where cost matters. It is available as claude-haiku-5-5 and on AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure. On Anthropic's own tests it scores 39.2% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and 72.4% on an offline subset of OSWorld 2.1, against 0.0% and 15.7% for Haiku 4.5; these figures are Anthropic's and have not been independently checked.
