OpenAI announced on 24 March 2026 that it would discontinue Sora in both its consumer app and its API, saying the Sora research team would concentrate on world-simulation research for robotics as compute demand grew elsewhere. The web and app experiences closed on 26 April, and OpenAI's help centre gives 24 September 2026 as the API's end date, after which associated user data is permanently deleted. Anyone who built on the Sora API needs another video model, such as Veo, Kling or Runway.

Source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001152-what-to-know-about-the-sora-discontinuation
