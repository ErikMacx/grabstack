Meta rolled out Muse on 8 September, describing it as a personal AI agent that works around the clock towards a user's goals. Meta says it can be instructed through its app or WhatsApp to send emails, book travel and buy things online, and that users choose which apps and services it can reach and can disconnect them at any time. It is one of the first mass-market agents from a major platform, and puts Meta in direct competition with OpenAI and Anthropic for the always-on assistant.
