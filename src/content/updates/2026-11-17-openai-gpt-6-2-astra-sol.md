OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra, for the hardest work, and GPT-6.2 Sol, for everyday work. The API names are `gpt-6.2-astra` and `gpt-6.2-sol`. Astra costs $8 input and $40 output per million tokens; Sol costs $2 and $10. Both are available to Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT and Codex, and in the API, with an Ultrafast option. The benchmark results, including comparisons with Claude Fable 5.2, are OpenAI's own. OpenAI says both models meet its "Critical" cybersecurity threshold, and that Astra is the first externally released model to meet its "High" threshold for AI self-improvement.
