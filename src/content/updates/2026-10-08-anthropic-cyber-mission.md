Anthropic announced the Anthropic Cyber Mission on 8 October. Its Critical Infrastructure Defense Program gives providers such as Accenture, Booz Allen, CrowdStrike and Rockwell Automation access to frontier Claude models, on-site engineers and threat research for operational technology. It also offers OSS Scanner, a free, opt-in service that runs periodic vulnerability scans on enrolled open-source projects, and Anthropic says it has funded several open-source foundations and vulnerability-coordination groups.
