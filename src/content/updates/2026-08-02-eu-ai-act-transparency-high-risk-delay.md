The Digital Omnibus on AI was published in the EU's Official Journal on 24 July and entered into force on 27 July, moving the deadline for stand-alone high-risk AI systems from 2 August 2026 to 2 December 2027, and for AI built into regulated products to 2 August 2028. Transparency duties under Article 50, such as telling people they are dealing with an AI system, still applied from 2 August 2026, with a grace period to 2 December 2026 for marking synthetic content from systems already on the market. The Omnibus also adds a ban on AI systems used to generate non-consensual intimate imagery and child sexual abuse material. Anyone shipping chatbots or generative features into the EU needs disclosure and labelling in place now; the heavier high-risk regime has more than a year's reprieve.
