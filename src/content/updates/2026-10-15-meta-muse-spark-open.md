Meta released Muse Spark Open on 15 October. It is natively multimodal, with 816B total parameters, 42B active and a 1M-token context. Weights are on Hugging Face and the major cloud model catalogues, under the Muse Model License, which Meta says has limited commercial terms. Meta calls it the most capable US open-weight model to date and says it runs on two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios. These are Meta's own claims.
