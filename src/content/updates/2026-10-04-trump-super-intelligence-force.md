President Trump announced the Super Intelligence Force on social media on 4 October, saying it will coordinate federal efforts to ensure the US 'continues to lead the World in Super Intelligence' and handle the government's dealings with consumers, interest groups, critical infrastructure providers and AI companies. It is led by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton, FTC chair Andrew Ferguson, Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering Emil Michael and Office of Personnel Management director Scott Kupor. The announcement gave few details of its powers or oversight, and follows the voluntary White House accord signed on 29 September.
