At DevDay on 29 September, OpenAI introduced Dots, always-on agents in ChatGPT that run on GPT-6 Astra, each with its own cloud computer and access to more than 4,000 apps through plugins; they began rolling out to ChatGPT Pro and Business Premium subscribers, though not to Pro users in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK. GPT-6.1 Sol offers what OpenAI calls near-Astra intelligence for coding, computer use and professional work at one-fifth of Astra's API prices, at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. Other announcements included Codex in the cloud, computer use in the Agents API, Sign in with ChatGPT, ChatGPT Space and Pages, and a Meetings plugin.
