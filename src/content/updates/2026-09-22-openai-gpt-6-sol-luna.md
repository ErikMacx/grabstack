OpenAI expanded the GPT-6 family on 22 September with GPT-6 Sol, aimed at repeated complex work such as building features, reviewing code and analysing data, and GPT-6 Luna, for high-volume jobs such as summarisation and extraction. Both are priced at half or less of their GPT-5.6 equivalents on the API, while Astra remains the model for the hardest tasks. For agents that make many model calls, these cheaper tiers matter more to running costs than the flagship; OpenAI followed up a week later with GPT-6.1 Sol.
