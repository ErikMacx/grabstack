Anthropic introduced Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1 on 1 September, calling them its most advanced models for coding and knowledge work. Fable 5.1 is available to subscribers and on the API at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens, with cache reads cut to $0.25 per million; Anthropic says typical workloads cost about 25% less and agentic ones up to 45% less, though early independent tests were mixed. Mythos 5.1 remains restricted to vetted organisations through Anthropic's Glasswing programme. Fable 5.1 became the model that later launches, including GPT-6 Astra, were benchmarked against.
