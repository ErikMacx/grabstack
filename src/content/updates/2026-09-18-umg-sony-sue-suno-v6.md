Universal Music Group and Sony Music Entertainment filed a second lawsuit against Suno on 18 September in federal court in Boston. They allege Suno copied 60,202 of their sound recordings without a licence, got round YouTube's download protections, and built its v6 models partly on the output of earlier models, which they call 'the fruit of the same poisoned tree'. The case tests whether training on an earlier model's output can clean up an infringing dataset, a question that reaches well beyond music.
