Google announced Gemini 4 Carbon on 28 October, two weeks after Argon. It is rolling out first to a subset of cyber defenders and then to more trusted partners through the Fairwind Program. Google gives no general release date. It says Carbon ranks first on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 with a score of 59, and lists the introductory price as $2/$10 per million tokens, rising to $4/$20. The security and safety figures are Google's own.
