Mistral's news page lists the release of Le Chonk's weights on 29 October, described as an open model for agentic coding and cybersecurity. Mistral Large 4 first appeared as an API preview on 6 October, with about 1 trillion parameters in total and 52 billion active. The licence was not stated on the pages we could open, and we could not open the 29 October article itself. The benchmark figures on the launch page are mostly Mistral's own, with some from third parties.
