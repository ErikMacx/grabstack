Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5.5 on 28 September as a faster, lower-cost complement to Opus 5.5, aimed at well-scoped everyday tasks, bug fixing and producing documents, slides and spreadsheets. It costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, and is available on Anthropic's platform as claude-sonnet-5-5 and on AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure. Anthropic says Claude Haiku 5.5 will complete the family in the coming weeks.
