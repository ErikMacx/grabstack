Google announced Gemini 4 Argon on 30 September, saying it delivers frontier performance in software engineering, legal and financial knowledge work, and cyber defence. Google is releasing it in phases, starting with trusted cyber defenders through its Fairwind Program and pre-release access for US government processes, and widening access as it gathers feedback. Argon will launch at an introductory price of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. Like GPT-6 Astra and Claude Mythos 5.1, its rollout shows frontier labs now gating their strongest models on cyber risk.
