In an essay titled 'We Must Pace the Frontier', published on 12 September, Amodei wrote that 'we must slow the pace at which we improve the capabilities of AI models', arguing that progress is outrunning researchers' ability to understand and control it. He proposed independent evaluators embedded inside labs, coordination among frontier companies in democracies with government help, and wider international coordination, which he estimated could buy one to two years for safety work. Sam Altman and Elon Musk publicly backed the call within days, while President Trump said 'whoever wins AI, wins'.
