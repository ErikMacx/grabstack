DeepSeek listed DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 on Hugging Face under the MIT licence. The model card calls it a minor update to V4.1-Flash, with a recommended 1M-token context. DeepSeek's own results include Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 42.3 (up from 31.2) and DeepSWE v1.1 at 77.0 (up from 74.2). The card shows Claude Opus 5 ahead on most of the tests it lists. The card gives no release date, so 17 October is taken from the model's listing.
